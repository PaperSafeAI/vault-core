# Community conduct

Everyone participating in vault-core should be able to contribute safely and
respectfully. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions
and other project spaces, including project-related private communication.

Be constructive, explain technical disagreements, respect experience levels
and accept correction. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, sexually harass,
impersonate others or publish another person's private information. Do not
publish vulnerability details before coordinated disclosure; use SECURITY.md.

Report conduct concerns privately to security@papersafeai.com. Include relevant
links and only the information needed to assess the concern. Maintainers will
handle reports discreetly, share information only as necessary to investigate,
and explain applicable actions. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
Retaliation against someone raising a concern is unacceptable.

Maintainers may request corrections, remove harmful content, temporarily
restrict participation or permanently exclude repeated or serious misconduct.
Responses should reflect severity and context. Reporters and affected people
may ask for reconsideration through the same address; a different available
maintainer should review that request where possible.

This policy covers project participation, not an obligation to accept code or
a guarantee of response times. Vulnerability-report expectations are in SECURITY.md.
